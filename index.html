Statistics and Cryptography Homeworks

Explore cryptography, stochastic processes, and the behavior of random samples.

Homework 1a: Private key to public key

Use a toy elliptic curve to turn a private key into a public point and inspect the results.

Homework 1b: From mnemonic to Ethereum address

Follow the real wallet pipeline from a mnemonic to an Ethereum address.

Homework 2: Encryption and cryptanalysis

Encrypt a message with secp256k1 and Caesar, recover keys, and measure how text length affects statistical attacks.

Homework 3: Penetration, LLN and CLT

Simulate attackers across systems and compare totals, averages, and normalized scores under Bernoulli and Rademacher models.

Homework 4: Sampling mean and variance

Derive Welford’s recurrences and explore how hackers’ score estimates concentrate as sample sizes grow.

Homework 5: Integration and the normal CDF

Compare Riemann and Lebesgue-style integration, then compute the standard normal CDF numerically.

Homework 6: Statistical independence

Build contingency tables, compare conditional and marginal frequencies, and test independence with chi-square.

Homework 7: Convergence and Bayesian inference

Compare modes of convergence in the LLN and CLT, then update priors into posteriors with Bayes' theorem.
